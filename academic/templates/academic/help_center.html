How do I change my email address?

{% if request.user.is_admin or request.user.role == 'ADMIN' %}

As an administrator, your account is exempt from the institutional email restriction. You can manage your email address directly through your Profile Settings or the System Control panel.

{% else %}

Your email address is permanently tied to your institutional `.edu` or `.edu.bd` account for verification purposes. If you lose access to your university email, contact the system administrator.

{% endif %}

Why are my GitHub commits not showing up?

Ensure your GitHub username is correctly entered in your Profile Settings. Also, confirm that your local Git configuration (`git config user.name`) matches your name or GitHub username. Note: Merge commits are intentionally ignored.

How does Peer Verification work?

When you submit a deliverable, you add a claim for what you worked on. Your teammates will receive a notification to verify or reject it. A single rejection flags the claim for instructor review.

{% endif %} {% if request.user.is_instructor or request.user.role == 'INSTRUCTOR' %}

How do I request a custom grading policy?

Navigate to the specific Project Dashboard and click "Request Policy Change" near the grading weights. Your Course Coordinator will review and approve the request via their inbox.

How are free-riders detected?

The Assessment Hub automatically flags students with massive code dumps (>1,000 lines at once), fluff commits, or those whose peer evaluation scores deviate heavily from their Git footprint.

{% endif %} {% if request.user.is_coordinator or request.user.role == 'COORDINATOR' %}

How do I enforce a global curriculum policy?

Go to Curriculum Policies and edit the course weights. This sets the default GitHub, Deliverable, and Peer Evaluation weights for all sections taught by instructors under this course.
